## Summary

DengueWatch BD does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything you enter, and
your location, stay on your phone.

## What the app does

DengueWatch BD shows publicly published dengue surveillance figures for
Bangladesh from the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), a symptom
guide based on WHO dengue warning-sign criteria, a directory of hospitals, and
prevention information.

## Information stored on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It is not uploaded, and it is excluded
from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied
to your Google account.

- **Your chosen area** and app settings, such as your language and alert
  preferences.
- **Your symptom log**, if you choose to save entries from the symptom check.
  This is stored in the app's private storage on the device.
- **Your location**, when you grant permission. It is used only on the phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Location

Location is optional. The app works without it; you can choose your area
manually instead.

When granted, location is used on the device for three purposes:

1. To show the dengue risk reading for the area you are actually in.
2. To find hospitals near you, by handing your search to your maps app.
3. To notice when you enter an area in the high or severe risk band, and warn
   you.

The third purpose requires **background location**. Android must be able to
check your area while the app is closed, or an entry warning could only ever
appear when you already had the app open, which defeats its purpose. You are
asked for this separately, only after you switch the warning on, and you can
turn it off at any time in the app or in Android's settings.

Your location is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no server
that receives it.

## Network connections

The app makes only these requests, and none of them carry your personal data:

| Host | Purpose | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| `druzzal.github.io` | Downloads the public dengue figures | A plain request for a public JSON file. No parameters, no body. |
| `tiles.openfreemap.org` | Map tiles for the risk map | Requests for the map tiles being displayed. |

As with any internet request, these hosts can see your device's IP address and
which files you requested, which is inherent to how the internet works. We do
not receive that information — there is no server operated by this app.

## Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no
analytics SDKs and no social media SDKs.

## Children

The app is general-purpose public-health information. It does not knowingly
collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not
collect information at all.

## Medical disclaimer

DengueWatch BD is an information tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything.
The symptom check follows WHO dengue warning-sign criteria to help you decide
when to seek care; it is not a substitute for seeing a clinician.

The figures come from DGHS and count patients reported by hospitals, so they
undercount anyone who had dengue but never sought hospital care.

DengueWatch BD is an independent app that uses official DGHS data. It is not
made by, or endorsed by, DGHS.
