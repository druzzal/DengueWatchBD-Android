## Full description (4000 char max)

    DengueWatch BD shows where dengue is spreading in Bangladesh, using the
    official figures published by the Directorate General of Health Services
    (DGHS).

    WHAT IT SHOWS

    • Today's dengue risk for your area, banded from the last two weeks of
      reported cases per 100,000 residents.
    • A map of all reporting areas, sized by season total or by rate, so you
      can compare a city corporation with a whole division fairly.
    • National trends — cases this season, this week, the last 24 hours, and
      how they compare with previous years.
    • Who is affected, by age and sex.

    CHECK YOUR SYMPTOMS

    A symptom guide built on WHO dengue warning-sign criteria. It does not
    diagnose dengue — nothing but a clinician and a blood test can — but it
    helps you decide whether to watch and wait, get tested, see a doctor
    today, or go to hospital now.

    FIND CARE

    Emergency numbers that work anywhere in Bangladesh, and the government
    hospitals that run dengue wards during the season, listed by division.

    PREVENT IT

    When the dengue season usually rises and falls, how to find and destroy
    breeding sites around your home, and how to avoid bites during the day,
    when Aedes mosquitoes bite.

    ALERTS

    Choose your area and get told when its risk band rises. Optionally, be
    warned when you enter a high-risk area.

    IN BANGLA AND ENGLISH

    Every screen, every figure and every alert, in either language. Bengali
    numerals throughout when you choose Bangla.

    PRIVACY

    No account. No analytics. No advertising. Your area, your settings and
    your symptom log stay on your phone, and are excluded from cloud backup.
    Location, if you allow it, is used only on the device.

    ABOUT THE DATA

    Figures come from the DGHS Health Emergency Operation Center dashboard and
    are refreshed automatically. They count patients reported by hospitals, so
    they undercount anyone who had dengue but never sought hospital care.

    DengueWatch BD is an independent app that uses official DGHS data. It is
    not made by, or endorsed by, DGHS.
